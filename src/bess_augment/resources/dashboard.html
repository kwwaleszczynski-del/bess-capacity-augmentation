Keeping an ageing battery in the Polish capacity market

A 1 MW lithium iron phosphate battery with a capacity-market contract loses energy as it ages. The charts show when its available energy falls below what the obligation requires and what each way of keeping it there costs: overbuild at the start, augmentation, restricted operation or module replacement. Ageing model fitted to open cell data, arbitrage revenue from PSE RCE prices (Oct 2025 - Sep 2026). Not investment or legal advice.
